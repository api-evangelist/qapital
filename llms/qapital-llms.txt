# Qapital

> Qapital is a consumer personal-finance company founded in 2013, "born in Stockholm, raised in
> New York City". It sells a mobile membership app that combines automated saving (Goals + Rules),
> collaborative saving (Dream Team), budgeting, investing through SEC-registered advisory and
> brokerage partners, and a Qapital Visa debit card on FDIC-member partner banking. Qapital reports
> 3.5m+ downloads and about $3B collectively saved by members.

## Agent-relevant summary

Qapital has NO public API. As of 2026-08-26 there is no developer portal, no API reference, no
OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK, no CLI, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, and no
/.well-known/ document of any kind. `developer.qapital.com` and `docs.qapital.com` do not resolve.
`api.qapital.com` resolves to an AWS ELB but answers every anonymous request — including
/openapi.json, /swagger.json, /docs, /graphql and /.well-known/* — with HTTP 503 Service
Unavailable; it is the private backend for the mobile apps, not a published product.

The only public programmable surface is a Qapital service on IFTTT with four polling triggers,
three Pro+ queries and one action. Third-party read access to Qapital account data is generally
obtained through open-banking aggregators rather than from Qapital directly.

## Product

- [Save](https://www.qapital.com/saving/): Goals and the Rules automation engine (round-up, guilty pleasure, payday divvy, freelancer tax set-aside, activity triggers).
- [Invest](https://www.qapital.com/invest/): Risk-weighted, auto-rebalanced portfolios via Qapital Invest, LLC.
- [Budget](https://www.qapital.com/budget/): Payday budgeting and weekly spending targets.
- [Dream Team](https://www.qapital.com/dream-team/): Two-person collaborative saving and shared spend tracking.
- [Qapital Visa Debit Card](https://www.qapital.com/qapital-visa-debit-card/): Spending account and card issued by Lincoln Savings Bank, Member FDIC.

## Trust and security

- [Security](https://www.qapital.com/security/): SOC 2 Type I examination completed; TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest; internal MFA and least-privilege; third-party penetration testing; FDIC insurance up to $250,000 through member partner banks.
- Domain posture probed 2026-08-26: TLS 1.3, HSTS max-age 63072000, DNSSEC signed, CAA records present, SPF published, DMARC p=reject.
- No /.well-known/security.txt (404), no published coordinated-disclosure policy, and no bug-bounty program. Security questions go to support@qapital.com.

## Integrations

- [Qapital on IFTTT](https://ifttt.com/qapital): Triggers — create or join a new goal; every time you save; every time you save using a specific method; every time you save toward a specific goal. Queries (IFTTT Pro+) — history of saves, history of saves by method, history of goals created or joined. Action — save toward a goal.
